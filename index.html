Chaupar — the Cloth Cross Race

Pachisi’s courtly sibling: bring all four of your lacquered gothi around the embroidered cross and into the Charkoni. Murky throws, gracious captures.

Chaupad honours the anthology of Native & Indigenous Sports of India (CUTM, 2025) — a cloth cross race, drawn on khadi silk, thrown with pasha long-dice.

How to Play Chaupad

Objective. First player (or partnership) to bring every lacquered gothi around the cross and into the central Charkoni wins. Others finish in rank order.

  • The cloth. Four arms of embroidered velvet-cloth squares run from a central 3×3 sanctuary. Each player owns a side: ◆ Sindoor South, ▲ Mayur West, ● Haldi North, ★ Neel East.
  • Throwing. Three long pasha sticks, each carved with 1, 2, 5 or 6. The total of the three is your step count.
  • Moving. Tap a glowing gothi to preview its landing square, then confirm. One new gothi may enter from the tray on any throw.
  • Grace (चौ). A triple of equal sticks grants a bonus throw as well as your move.
  • Capture. Landing on an enemy on any square that is not a castle sends it home and grants a bonus throw. Castles are marked with the gold star-and-cross.
  • Home. Reaching the top of your own arm by an exact land turns the gothi inward; descend your private column to the Charkoni. Any throw equal to or greater than the last steps brings it home.
  • Partnerships. In 2v2, opposite players are allies; they may share squares, and the team wins when all eight gothis are home.
