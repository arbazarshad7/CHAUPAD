Chaupad Tutorial

1

The Cloth Cross & 4 Royal Houses

Welcome to Chaupad, India’s regal cloth-cross race game. You command four lacquered wooden pieces (gothi). Your quest is to march all four pieces along the cross and guide them into the central lotus sanctuary called the Charkoni.

👆 Tap any house to inspect
◆ Sindoor South Arm (Player): Commands 4 vermilion pieces from the southern yard into battle.
💡
First Rule: You can bring a fresh gothi out from your yard tray onto your home gate on any throw! No special roll of 1 or 6 is needed to enter.
2

Throwing the Pasha (Long-Dice)

Unlike Western cubes, Chaupad is played with three rectangular wooden sticks called pasha. Each stick has four carved faces: 1, 2, 5, and 6 (there are no 3s or 4s!).

👆 Tap sticks or button to roll
Total: 12
✨
The sum of all three sticks (between 3 and 18) is your movement count. Tap a highlighted piece on your turn to preview where it lands!
3

Grace Throws (चौ • Chau / Triples)

When all three sticks reveal the exact same face (such as 1-1-1, 2-2-2, 5-5-5, or 6-6-6), the court declares a Grace Throw (चौ)!

👆 Tap below to test a live Grace throw
★ चौ • CHAU • BONUS THROW ★
Three identical sticks grant you your move PLUS an extra throw!
⚡
Bonus Chain: Up to 3 consecutive bonus throws can be chained in a single turn through Grace rolls or enemy captures!

Home Column & Charkoni Victory

After completing the outer circuit of all four arms, your gothi arrives back at your home gate:

👆 Tap to celebrate reaching home
🌸 Descending into the Lotus Charkoni

1. Reaching your gate by an exact count turns your gothi inward down your private column.
2. Any throw equal to or greater than remaining steps carries the piece safely home into the central Charkoni.
3. The first player to bring all 4 gothi home triumphs over the court!
